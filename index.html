The Connected Jewelry OS

One system for the counter, the bench, the books and every client.

2SDAY AM replaces the stack of separate programs a jewelry store runs on — point of sale, clienteling, repairs, custom design, texting and bookkeeping — with one connected operating system your whole team works in.

The platform

Four parts, one connected system

Staff

The register, repairs, custom orders, layaways and stock in one place — each associate on their own PIN, each sale on the right person.

  • Checkout with sales, repairs and special orders on one ticket
  • Repair intake with photos on the claim ticket
  • Stock catalog, tags, counts and RFID
  • Payroll, commission and the books

Clients

Every client's purchases, repairs, sizes, dates and conversations on one card — and a private Vault where they see their own collection.

  • Complete purchase and repair history
  • Texting from your store's own number, with consent kept
  • The client Vault: wishlist, private viewings, events
  • Appointments from your booking calendar

Automation

The follow-ups, reminders and paperwork that slip through the cracks, handled on schedule and only during business hours.

  • Pickup, layaway and appointment reminders
  • Vendor statements checked against what arrived
  • Package tracking with alerts
  • A day-close digest for managers

Relationships

Tools for the moments that matter — designing the ring, presenting it, and remembering the story long after the sale.

  • Design Studio with live 3-D and try-on
  • Guided presentations and coaching
  • Thank-you letters and anniversary reminders
  • Ask Joe: plain-English answers about the store
