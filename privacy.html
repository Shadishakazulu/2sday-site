Privacy notice

Draft — to be reviewed by counsel before launch.

This notice covers the 2sday.am company website. Each store running on 2SDAY AM has its own address and its own client records; how a store uses its clients' information is covered by that store's own privacy notice.

What we collect here

When you request a demo we receive the details you type into the form: your name, store name, email, phone (if given), number of locations and your message. When you use "Sign in to your store", the store you choose is remembered only in your own browser so it's there next time.

How we use it

Only to contact you about a demo of 2SDAY AM. We don't sell it or share it for advertising.

Questions

Contact us to see, correct or delete what you sent us.
